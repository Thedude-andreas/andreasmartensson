Privacy policy

This privacy policy applies to AMC Download Manager for Android, published by Andreas Mårtensson.

Summary

AMC Download Manager does not collect, upload, sell, or remotely store personal data. The app has no account system, advertising, analytics, crash-reporting service, or tracking SDK. File operations are performed locally on your Android device.

File access and purpose

The app's core purpose is to automatically find the most recently downloaded file and provide local file-management actions. To do this, it requests access to shared storage, including the public Downloads folder.

The permission is used to list file names and metadata, open files with compatible apps, and carry out share, rename, and delete actions initiated by you. Extra folders are accessed only after you select them with Android's system folder picker.

Collection and sharing

No file, file name, metadata, folder selection, or other personal data is sent to the developer or to a third party by AMC Download Manager. A file leaves the device only when you explicitly select Share and then choose a destination app in Android's share interface. That destination app's own privacy policy then applies.

Local storage and retention

The app stores references to extra folders you selected in local app preferences so access can remain available between launches. This information stays on the device and is removed when you remove the folder in the app or uninstall the app. App preferences are excluded from cloud backup and device transfer.

Your controls

  • Revoke All files access in Android Settings.
  • Remove individually selected folders from the app's Folders dialog.
  • Uninstall the app to remove its local preferences.
  • Choose whether to share, rename, or delete any file.

Children

The app is a general file-management utility and is not designed or marketed specifically for children.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, this policy and the app's disclosures will be updated before the changed version is distributed.
